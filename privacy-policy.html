Sudoku Oasis — Privacy Policy

Updated: 18 November 2026 · Developer: Amr Arnous · Contact: amrarnous15@gmail.com

Sudoku Oasis ("the app") is a free Sudoku game supported by advertising. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information the app stores on your device

Your saved boards, entries, notes, personal statistics and settings are stored on your device. Android may include these in your device backup. Uninstalling removes the local copy.

The app does not ask for an account, your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Device identifiers — the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address — which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
  • Product interactions — such as app launches, taps and ad video views;
  • Diagnostic information — such as app performance and launch time.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional gameplay analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Analytics is off unless you allow it. If you opt in, Google processes a pseudonymous app-instance identifier, device and app information, approximate region inferred from your IP address, app opens, sessions and engagement. We use reports on puzzle starts, first moves and outcomes by difficulty and game mode to understand whether people actually play. We do not send your board, notes or digits, or set a user ID. Analytics does not use your advertising ID for these reports.

You can decline on the home screen or turn off Settings → Gameplay analytics at any time without affecting play. This stops further analytics collection; it does not delete reports already collected. Your ad choices are separate.

Google Play reviews

After several wins, Google Play may invite you to rate the game. This is optional. Play handles your rating and any review under its privacy policy. The game does not receive your rating or review text from the prompt.

Your choices and consent

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and regions with similar laws, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded videos (for extra hints or a second chance) are always optional.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (PG). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Clearing app storage removes your local progress and choices, not past analytics reports. Analytics event-level data is retained under the retention setting of our Google Analytics property. Contact us with privacy questions. For data associated with your Google account, see Google’s privacy controls.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions? Email amrarnous15@gmail.com.

يمكنك الرفض من الشاشة الرئيسية أو إيقاف «إحصاءات استخدام اللعبة» من الإعدادات في أي وقت. لن يؤثر ذلك في اللعب أو يغيّر خيارات الإعلانات. يتوقف جمع البيانات الجديدة عند إيقاف الميزة، لكن التقارير السابقة لا تُحذف. تخضع مدة الاحتفاظ ببيانات الأحداث لإعدادات الاحتفاظ في «إحصاءات Google». يؤدي مسح بيانات التطبيق إلى حذف تقدّمك وخياراتك من الجهاز فقط.
