Sudoku Oasis — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Developer: Amr Arnous · Contact: amrarnous15@gmail.com

Sudoku Oasis ("the app") is a free Sudoku game supported by advertising. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress, statistics (times, wins, streaks) and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers and do not receive this information. If you use Android backup, this data may be included in your device backup managed by Google. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The app does not ask for an account, your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Device identifiers — the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address — which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
  • Product interactions — such as app launches, taps and ad video views;
  • Diagnostic information — such as app performance and launch time.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices and consent

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and regions with similar laws, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded videos (for extra hints or a second chance) are always optional.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (PG). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data. Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. For data held by Google, use your device's ad ID controls or Google's privacy tools.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions? Email amrarnous15@gmail.com.
